WEALTH OS local exercise animations

These eight GIFs (squat, pushup, curl, lateral raise, lunge, plank, deadlift, jumping jack) are original schematic instructional animations generated for this personal project. They are intentionally schematic, not recordings of real people, and are for movement orientation only.

They are stored locally under /static/animations/ and play without a third-party request. Actions without a matching local animation display an icon and are explicitly marked as not having a local GIF; no unrelated animation is substituted.
